Download Speed

Measures how quickly your connection fetches data from web servers to your machine. Critical for high-resolution video streaming, large file downloads, and browsing responsiveness.

Upload Speed

Measures the speed at which you send data outward. Vital for Zoom/Teams video conferencing, live streaming on YouTube/Twitch, cloud backups, and sending email attachments.

Latency & Jitter

Ping indicates response time in milliseconds (ms). Jitter represents the consistency of that response time. Lower ping and minimal jitter prevent packet drops and gaming lag.

Frequently Asked Questions

Why does my tested speed differ from my ISP subscription plan? ▾

Internet Service Providers advertise maximum "up to" theoretical speeds. Real-world speeds can be affected by Wi-Fi interference, router hardware limits, background downloads, or congestion during peak network hours.

What is the difference between Megabits (Mbps) and Megabytes (MB/s)? ▾

There are 8 bits in a byte. Internet speeds are universally measured in Megabits per second (Mbps), while file download sizes on your computer are measured in Megabytes (MB). A 100 Mbps line downloads files at roughly 12.5 MB/s.

Does this speed test store or log my personal data? ▾

No. The engine runs completely client-side in your web browser. Benchmark logs are stored only in your local browser storage (localStorage) and can be cleared at any time.
